Observability Portal

Enable

turn observability on — guided, pre-filled from the CMDB
  • Add Monitoring — CMD, log & process alerts, routed to the right platform automatically
  • ELK Log Onboarding — agents, Kafka topic, parsers, indices from one request

Assure

  • Observability Score — a scorecard and certification level per asset
  • Synthetic Alerting — fire-drill an alert end to end, ticket included
  • Reconciliation Reports — expected vs reality across ELK, DX UIM, SolarWinds

Two systems of record

CMDB / Asset Registry

what exists

Every host and service, its entity, environment, and owner. The portal resolves assets from here — users type a name, never re-enter facts.

Git — the Second Brain

what is configured, and why

All monitoring config, docs, and decisions as versioned plain text. It powers the Brain's answers, the scorecards, and the audit trail — and makes the estate AI-ready.
